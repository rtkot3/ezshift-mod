# r3 Extension for EZShift 🗓️

![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-3.4-purple)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-green)
![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-EZShift-blue)

**EZShift r3 Mod** is an advanced UserScript designed to supercharge the EZShift scheduling interface specifically developed for Euro Live Technologies. Tailored for employees and managers, it extracts schedule data on the fly to provide powerful insights without the need to reload pages or navigate complex menus.

### Key Features:

* **Quick Schedule Lookups:** Instantly search for colleagues and visualize their monthly shifts on a clean timeline.

* **Primary Team Analysis:** Automatically calculates a staff member's main department based on their shift history and color-codes the UI accordingly.

* **Smart 'Imposter' Tracking:** Dynamically filters dates and areas to help identify staff members covering shifts outside their primary responsibilities.

* **Seamless UI Integration:** Injects a responsive, non-intrusive control panel directly into the native EZShift sidebar.
